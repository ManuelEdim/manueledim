### About

I sit at the join between applied ML research and shipped software: co-authoring peer-reviewed work on explainable and evidence-calibrated ML systems by day, and building React/Supabase products under my dev studio, **Emediahome**, the rest of the time. Supervised by Prof. Celestine Iwendi at CIoTh, University of Greater Manchester.

Recurring thread across my research: **models that admit their limits are more useful than models that overclaim.** Several of the papers below are explicitly "honest-null" or "predictable but not suppressible" results — reframing a negative or partial finding as an architecture or measurement contribution rather than hiding it.

### Research
